How it works

Three steps to get paid

Sign up, generate a payment link, share it with your customer. Funds arrive in your wallet automatically every morning.

01
👤
Create your account

Sign up with your email and Solana wallet address. Your merchant escrow vault is registered on-chain automatically in the background — no manual steps.

02
⊕
Generate a payment link

Enter an amount (or leave it open) and get a QR code plus a shareable link instantly. Send it via WhatsApp, email, or embed it on your website.

03
$
Receive funds daily

Payments go straight into your on-chain escrow vault. Every day at 6am UTC, SETTL automatically releases your balance to your wallet, net of 1.5%.

Features

Everything a merchant needs

From simple QR payments to full API integration — SETTL covers every use case.

📱
Solana Pay QR

Customers scan with Phantom, Backpack, Solflare or any Solana wallet. No app needed on your side.

🔗
Reusable links

Create a permanent link for your tip jar, product page, or invoice. Each visitor gets a fresh session automatically.

⌁
Webhooks

Get an HTTP POST to your server the moment a payment confirms. HMAC-signed so you know it's real. 3 retries on failure.

⌘
API keys

Create payment sessions from your own backend. One API call returns a checkout URL you redirect your customer to.

🎨
Checkout branding

Set your business name, logo, and brand colour. Your customers see your brand, not SETTL's, on the checkout page.

↺
Daily auto-release

No manual withdrawals. Every day at 6am UTC, your escrow balance is automatically released to your wallet on-chain.
